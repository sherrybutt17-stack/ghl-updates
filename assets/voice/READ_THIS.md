# Voice recording: read this out loud (60–90 seconds)

**Setup:** quiet room with soft furnishings (a bedroom or closet is better than a kitchen).
Turn off fans and AC. Phone Voice Memos (Settings → Voice Memos → Audio Quality: **Lossless**)
or a USB mic, held **15–20 cm** from your mouth, at the same distance the whole time.
Stay silent for 1 second, then read in the **same upbeat "presenter" voice you want in the videos**.
Don't rush, and don't worry about small stumbles; just keep going.
